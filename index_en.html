Candle Care Guide

Handcrafted soy wax candles behave differently than paraffin. Follow these tips to enjoy an optimal clean burn.

01

Keep the Wick Trimmed

Before lighting your candle, trim the wick to about 0.5 cm. This prevents the flame from getting too high, producing black soot, or consuming the wax too quickly.

02

Candle Memory

The first time you light your candle, let it burn for 2 to 3 hours, until the entire top surface of wax melts completely. This prevents 'tunneling'.

03

Safe Extinguishment

Do not blow out your soy candle. Use a metal snuffer or smother the flame by placing the jar's lid. This avoids shifting the wick or producing heavy smoke that ruins the aroma.
